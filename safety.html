Safety questions

You asked, we answer.

How are drivers verified?

Before approval, every driver submits their Ghana Card, driver's licence and vehicle details. Our team reviews each application, and drivers can be re-checked over time.

What happens if I feel unsafe?

Use the in-app SOS button to reach emergency help with your live location attached, and contact Rydon support. You can also share your live trip with someone you trust before you set off.

Are trips insured?

Yes — every Rydon trip is covered. If you have questions about a specific incident, contact support@rydonghana.com.

How do I report a driver or rider?

Rate the trip and add a note, or contact support directly. Serious reports are investigated, and accounts can be suspended or removed.
