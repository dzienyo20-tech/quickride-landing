Good to know

Ride questions,
answered.

How do I pay for my ride?

Pay cash directly to your driver on arrival, or in-app with MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AirtelTigo Money or card. No prepayment is required for cash rides.

Do I see the price before booking?

Yes. Rydon shows your fare upfront before you confirm. In heavy traffic the final amount can move within a clear range so your driver is paid fairly for the extra time.

Which cities is Rydon in?

Rydon is launching across Ghana — from Accra and Tema to Kumasi, Takoradi and beyond. Open the app to see availability near you.

Is it safe?

Every driver is identity-verified. You can share your live trip with family and reach an in-app SOS and support at any time. See how we keep you safe.
