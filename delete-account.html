Account management

Delete your account.

You can permanently delete your Rydon account and personal data at any time — from the app or by email. Here's how, and what happens to your data.

This action is permanent. Once deleted, your account cannot be recovered. Please make sure you have no outstanding trips, payments or disputes before you delete.

Option 1 — Delete in the app (immediate)

  • Open the Rydon app and sign in.
  • Go to Account → Data & Privacy → Delete Account.
  • Confirm when prompted, then tap Delete Permanently.
  • Your account and personal data are deleted immediately and you are signed out — no waiting period.

Option 2 — Request by email

If you can't access the app, email us from the address or phone linked to your account and we'll handle it for you:

Email a deletion request

Email requests are processed within 30 days; you'll get a confirmation once complete.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile — name, email, phone number and photo
  • Saved payment methods
  • Location history and saved places
  • In-app messages and notifications
  • Ratings and reviews you've given
  • Driver verification documents (after the mandatory retention period)

What we retain

Some records must be kept for legal and regulatory compliance, linked to an anonymised record rather than to you:

  • Trip records and payment receipts — kept for 7 years for tax and legal purposes under Ghanaian law.
  • Driver verification documents — kept for 5 years after closure where required by law.
  • Fraud and safety records — kept where necessary to protect other users.
