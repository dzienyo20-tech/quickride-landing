Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 8, 2026

QuickRide Ghana ("QuickRide", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Name, phone number, email address, and profile photo when you register.
  • Location Data: Real-time GPS location to match you with nearby drivers, calculate routes, and track trips. Location is collected while the app is in use.
  • Payment Information: Mobile money number and transaction details processed through Paystack. We do not store full payment credentials.
  • Trip Data: Pickup and dropoff locations, trip duration, fare, and ratings.
  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, app version, and push notification token.
  • Identity Documents (Drivers only): Ghana Card, driver's license, vehicle registration, roadworthy certificate, and selfie photos for verification.
  • Communications: Messages between passengers and drivers through the in-app chat.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve our ride-hailing services
  • To match passengers with available drivers
  • To process payments and maintain transaction records
  • To verify driver identity and vehicle documents
  • To send trip updates, receipts, and promotional notifications
  • To ensure safety and prevent fraud
  • To comply with legal obligations in Ghana
  • To resolve disputes between passengers and drivers

3. Location Information

QuickRide requires access to your device's location to function. For passengers, location is used to show your position on the map and find nearby drivers. For drivers, location is continuously shared with passengers during active trips for safety and navigation purposes. You may disable location access in your device settings, but this will prevent you from using the app.

4. Sharing Your Information

We share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • With Drivers/Passengers: Your name, photo, rating, and real-time location are shared with your matched driver or passenger during a trip.
  • With Paystack: Payment information is shared with Paystack, our payment processor, to complete transactions.
  • With Cloudinary: Document photos are stored securely via Cloudinary.
  • With Law Enforcement: We may disclose information when required by Ghanaian law or legal process.
  • For Safety: In emergencies involving risk to life, we may share information with authorities.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active. Trip records are retained for 7 years for legal and tax purposes. Driver verification documents are retained for 5 years after account closure. You may request deletion of your account by contacting support@quickrideghana.com.

6. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS), hashed passwords, and secure cloud storage. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

7. Children's Privacy

QuickRide is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Opt out of marketing communications
  • Withdraw consent for location tracking (may limit app functionality)

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@quickrideghana.com.

9. Cookies and Analytics

Our mobile app does not use browser cookies. We may use analytics tools to understand app usage patterns and improve our service. Analytics data is anonymized and aggregated.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or by email. Your continued use of QuickRide after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

  • Email: privacy@quickrideghana.com
  • Support: support@quickrideghana.com
  • Website: quickrideghana.com
