The facts, fast.

Quick, accurate details for writing about Rydon. For logos, imagery or interviews, email us.

What Rydon is

Ghana's own ride-sharing and food-delivery app — car, okada and Comfort rides plus Rydon Foods, all in one app.

Ownership

Proudly Ghanaian-owned and independent, built for how Ghana moves.

Driver model

Drivers keep 90% of every fare, paid straight to Mobile Money.

Payments

MoMo-first: MTN Mobile Money, Telecel Cash and AirtelTigo Money, plus card and cash.

Coverage

Launching across Ghana — from Accra and Tema to Kumasi, Takoradi and beyond.
