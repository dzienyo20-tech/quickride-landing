How it works

Ride or drive —
three simple steps.

For riders

1

Download & sign up

Get Rydon from the App Store or Google Play. Sign up with your phone number — quick OTP, no email needed.

2

Set pickup & drop-off

Pin your locations and see the fare upfront. Choose a nearby verified driver in seconds.

3

Ride & pay your way

Track your driver live. Pay with MoMo, Telecel Cash, AirtelTigo, card or cash. Rate your driver after.

For drivers

1

Apply & complete KYC

Submit your Ghana Card, licence and vehicle photos. Our team reviews within 24 hours.

2

Go online & accept trips

Once approved, go online to receive nearby requests. Pick up and navigate with built-in maps.

3

Earn 90% — to your MoMo

Keep 90% of every fare. Earnings settle to your MTN MoMo wallet. Cash out any time.
